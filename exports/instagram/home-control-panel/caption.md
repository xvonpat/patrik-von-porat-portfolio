# Instagram: Home Control Panel

## Carousel (carousel-01 … carousel-09, 1080 × 1350)

A home full of different devices. One interface, made my way.

Lights, heating, the coffee machine, the car charger and the solar panels all came with their own apps. None of them showed the whole house, so I built one place for it on top of Home Assistant.

Rooms first, details one step further in. An air-comfort dot instead of raw numbers. A coffee machine that is warm on working days, and a hobby room that changes with what I am about to make.

I designed it and test every change at home; an AI coding assistant writes the code. It has been in daily use on my phone and computer since October.

The full story, including what happens after a tap: vonporat.com/projects (link in bio)
